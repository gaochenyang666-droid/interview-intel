# Chinese report specification

Reports contain a one-page summary and six numbered sections. When a quick input contains `0`, dependent company or role sections are skipped. Individual interviewer profiling is not part of the report.

1. `一页式摘要`
2. `1. 公司概况与综合评价`
3. `2. 员工及社交媒体评价`
4. `3. 岗位评价`
5. `4. 公开历史面经与匹配`
6. `5. 面试预测与准备`
7. `6. 来源与可信度`

## Language and evidence style

- Write in Simplified Chinese. Preserve source titles and verbatim interview questions in their original language.
- Use `事实`, `本人公开表述`, `第三方评价`, `分析推断`, `历史原题`, `相近岗位参考题`, `相似公司参考题`, `AI 预测题`, `信息缺口`, and `准备建议` consistently.
- Put evidence IDs next to every evidence-dependent statement. Put original URLs in the source appendix.
- Summaries must trace to finding IDs. Do not add facts in the summary that are absent from findings.
- Distinguish `未找到可靠公开证据` from `未检索` and from `页面无法访问`.
- Mention search date, intended languages, actual languages attempted, and inaccessible sources.
- Numeric company scoring stays off unless explicitly requested and validated through [scoring-rubric.md](scoring-rubric.md).

## Workplace experience

Section 2 must report which independent community platforms were actually searched, whether accessible original posts were found, and which sources were blocked or required login. Where verified originals exist, separate positive, negative, and mixed experiences and cite dates and URLs. An anonymous contributor's employment status is ordinarily unverified. Employer marketing does not substitute for independent workplace accounts.

If no public original can be opened, state `未核验到可公开访问的员工原帖或评价` and list attempted platforms. Do not invent employees, quotes, proportions, or sentiment.

## Historical interview experiences

Section 4 must separate:

- same company and same role;
- same company and related role;
- comparable company and same role;
- generic role predictions.

For each account, show location or level when known, post date and interview date when shown, rounds and format, verbatim reported questions, author-stated difficulty and impression, original evidence links, and limitations. Similar-company and related-role questions require migration rationale. Missing exact evidence leads to lower tiers or explicit gaps.

## Preparation and interactive practice

Section 5 contains ten questions with a role-sensitive mix. Each item must show:

1. question;
2. category;
3. source level and origin label;
4. original evidence link or explicit prediction basis;
5. competencies tested;
6. answer outline;
7. recommended answer structure.

The report ends the preparation section with an invitation to say `开始模拟面试`. The interactive mode shows one question at a time and hides answer guidance until feedback.

## Quality gate

Before presenting the report, confirm:

- Company identity is confirmed or clearly marked unresolved.
- No individual interviewer identities, biographies, or inferred preferences appear.
- Each fact-like conclusion has eligible evidence.
- Every URL came from an inspected page or user-supplied public URL.
- Historical questions exist in linked evidence; prediction and peer-company labels are accurate.
- Source dates and sample limitations are honest.
- Reposts are not counted as independent corroboration.
- Search snippets and inaccessible pages are not presented as verified facts.
- A mock interview evaluates only the current answers and never estimates real hiring probability.
